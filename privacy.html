Privacy Policy — 100KWhys Publisher

Last updated: November 6, 2026

100KWhys Publisher ("the App") is an internal publishing tool operated by the owner of the TikTok account @100kwhyssi and the YouTube channel 《深度解密十萬個為什麼》 (100KWhys). The App is used solely to upload and publish the owner's own original videos to the owner's own TikTok account through TikTok's official Content Posting API.

Information we collect

The App accesses only the TikTok account data required for authorization and posting: the account's basic profile information (open ID, display name) and an OAuth access token granted by the account owner. The App does not collect, store, or process data from any other TikTok user.

How we use information

The access token is stored securely on the operator's own computer and is used only to upload and publish the owner's videos and to check their posting status. We do not sell, share, transfer, or disclose any account data to third parties.

Data retention and deletion

The account owner may revoke the App's access at any time from TikTok account settings (Settings and privacy → Security and permissions → Apps and services), which immediately invalidates the stored token. Stored tokens can also be deleted on request by contacting us through the website below.
